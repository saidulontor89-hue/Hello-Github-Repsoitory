Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open Saturday to Thursday from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and on Friday from 12:00 PM to 11:30 PM. The kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available every day from 11:30 AM to 10:30 PM. Call us or send a message and we will confirm your area and delivery time.

Can I reserve a table for a group?

Of course. Send us a message with your preferred date, time and number of guests, and we will confirm your booking by phone or email.

How can I share feedback or report a problem?

Use the form above and choose "Feedback". We read every message and use it to improve our food and service.
